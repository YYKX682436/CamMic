BlackHole audio engine

The CamMic HAL render and capture engine is based on Existential Audio's BlackHole v0.7.1, upstream commit e2b22aaaba4e507a097131704bf96dabc004d9cf. Its source is licensed under GPL-3.0. The modified driver has CamMic branding and does not use the BlackHole logo or product branding.

Upstream source and license: BlackHole v0.7.1 pinned commit. Official upstream copyright and trademark notices are retained in the source and installed driver resources.

USB transport libraries

The CamMic Mac app bundles and dynamically links libusbmuxd 2.1.1, libimobiledevice-glue 1.3.3, and libplist 2.8.0 under LGPL-2.1-or-later. Their license texts and corresponding unmodified upstream source archives are included in CamMic.app/Contents/Resources/ and in the matching CamMic source archive. CamMic builds and distributes no iproxy or inetcat utility. The libusbmuxd source archive contains source for those separate GPL-2.0-or-later tools; its license text is also included in the app and installer resources.

  • libusbmuxd 2.1.1
  • libimobiledevice-glue 1.3.3
  • libplist 2.8.0
